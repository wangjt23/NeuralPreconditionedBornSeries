## What capture savings do to the budget

Let Cb be the complete successful classical cost, Cc the complete candidate cost, and g=.05. The available gate margin is

    H = (1-g) Cb - Cc.

It must pay every still-omitted encoder/prediction/loading/acquisition expense before a candidate is feasible. If improved capture saves db from the comparator and dc from the candidate, then

    Hnew - Hold = dc - (1-g) db.

An unchanged candidate therefore loses .95db of budget. If both inherit exactly the same saving d, the absolute advantage `Cb-Cc` is unchanged and the percentage-gate margin improves only by .05d. Do not assume dc=db: depths, captures, starts, calls, buffer ownership and acceptance work differ. If classical applications also become cheaper, eliminating one call buys less time. Lower common setup can slightly ease a relative gate while cheaper competing actions tighten correction economics.

The direct screen's setup-plus-warm accepted estimates exclude shared process startup/I/O and are not matched per-arm process-cold measurements. Constructor ordering is recorded; first-use effects remain visible. Charge the chosen backend's actual constructor, copies, initialization, complete solver and extra acceptance. A candidate inherits an optimization only when that same optimization is actually implemented and measured for it. Avoid subtracting overlapping synchronized profiles or treating standalone application times as universal lower bounds.

## Negative evidence that capture cannot erase

- Fixed-angular q3 whitening found tightly bracketed ordinary-FP64 raw-source residual floors around.60–.66. Its cached continuation failed the10% warm gate; prefix projected optima remained unresolved. Raw coercivity does not transfer through real-AZ projection without transversality. Cheaper capture does not repair missing angular range. [Whitening audit](../headroom/whitening_result_audit.md)
- The distinct full-response U range already exists as the classical homogeneous harmonic-input solve. StageA's96 accurate solves found TSA4 and its homogeneous q3 complement slower in all four Q1 contexts; removing response construction cost still did not rescue that tested composition. No heterogeneous CGLS coefficients or range ceiling were acquired. Smaller setup is not evidence that they would work. [Response decision](../response/NEXT_DIRECTION_REVIEW.md)
- Nonlinear/four-phase tails saved no mean strong-classical calls at N128/N256. Phase averaging did not establish complex linearity and did not repay inference. CT/NTO already cache material-conditioned linear factors; generic caching or linearity recommendations repeat existing work. A new late correction needs an explicit affordable progress witness on the actual deployed trajectory.

Angular reduction plus homogeneous FFT inversion is established by [Fan–An–Ying](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.03061). [Peng ROMSAD](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.08735v3) and [Tang–Peng trajectory-aware ROM](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.05001v1) are the closest kinetic-correction competitors; their discretization and acquisition/setup assumptions differ. [McMg](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.30495v2) supports material-cache/residual-linear multilevel design in Helmholtz, not a transport guarantee. No novelty claim follows from these ingredients or this ranking.

## Accuracy, economics and a concrete stopping interpretation

The authority remains the **original stored-buffer FP64 discrete periodic operator**, unchanged source-normalized true residual<=1e-5, evaluated relative error bound<=1e-3, and paired field difference<=1e-4. Exact backend parity supports the same computed solver action on tested inputs. The coercivity inequalities hold for the stated finite operator in exact arithmetic; evaluated phase eigenvalues, margins, residual gaps and error bounds are ordinary FP64 computations, **not directed-rounded certificates, continuum-RTE error bounds, or uniform convergence proofs**. Fixed L12/g.65/epsilon.1, reused development media and periodic boundaries limit the empirical scope.

Q1 remains primary. Q16 is conditional until distinct RHS reuse on the same material is measured; repeats or phase aliases do not create that workload. For training across media, charge

    T_offline + sum_m [S_candidate,m + Qm T_candidate,m]

against the strongest classical costs, crediting its factor/capture reuse too. Offline includes actual teacher/data/training/tuning; charge it once across its served workload, not once per medium and not zero. Negative online savings have no online amortization argument.

If the direct backend passes, freeze its benefit as classical credit, then pursue rank1's small geometry/cost discriminator. If that and a constrained replacement show no affordable headroom, record a concrete decision barrier for this fast workload instead of starting another blind training campaign. Harder absorption/angular/boundary regimes remain separate research questions requiring explicit workload scope and stronger corresponding classical controls.
