Verified full-depth replay retains the original 126 transforms at TSA16, passes all48 entry checks and all288 recorded full solves, and improves warm time on every development medium. Its mean extra capture/warmup cost is 122.534ms at N128 and186.229ms at N256. Mean original setup-plus-one-RHS estimates are145.034/159.859ms; full-depth graph estimates are171.835/294.244ms. These are recorded setup-plus-warm estimates, excluding shared process startup and distinct-source reuse, not process-cold measurements. [Verified audit](../headroom/graph_full16_audit.md)

For short capture let shared setup be S, short capture Cseg, and the entire accepted candidate solve Tseg. The >=5% Q1 gate is

    Cseg + Tseg <= 0.95 Cstar - S,

where Cstar is the lower successful complete eager/full-replay comparator cost in the same paired block. As illustration only, retaining the *old full-graph* warm times would allow roughly88.5ms capture at N128 and43.9ms at N256. Actual Tseg replaces this hypothesis; the existing warm time is not a bound. Never infer speed by subtracting synchronized profiling categories.

The original recurrence starts `z=0`, with first defect exactly rhs; subsequent stages use `rhs-Az`. A short captured subsequent-stage graph must retain the accumulated state without reading a stale output address. Any extra state copy is charged. Preserve the original additions and divisions; do not substitute the rejected algebraic inner defect or reciprocal multiplication. External frozen-material/layout/settings guards protect every application; output copies retain Krylov ownership. This route is compatible in principle with documented static CUDA-graph constraints, but its capture/parity/cost have not been demonstrated. [PyTorch CUDA graph documentation](https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/blob/v2.5.1/docs/source/notes/cuda.rst#cuda-graphs)

**Capture-floor control comes first.** Stage A records TSA4 graph construction133.7–175.5ms at N256 and135.7–140.4ms at N128 despite the shallow depth. Capture cost cannot be assumed proportional to stage count; two short graph pools might duplicate a substantial fixed cost. Measure an ownership-safe same-shape copy-only graph or a depth1 graph as an engineering cost control, then the actual first/subsequent capture. An eager first update plus a single subsequent-update graph avoids a second capture, at the price of extra launches each preconditioner call. Neither the control nor this alternative is an accuracy-qualified solver until parity and full-cost checks pass. Keep setup order and first-use effects explicit, with common warm-runtime and process-cold measurements reported separately if the latter are acquired.

## A precise cheap heterogeneous coefficient discriminator

With actual stored physics, `U(k)=H0(k)^-1 Q`, `G=Q*Q`, `Delta t=t-t0`, and `Delta s=s-s0`, define

    W(k)=Q*U(k),  Z(k)=Q*P U(k),
    C y = Q*H(Uy) = G y + Delta t(x) Wop y - Delta s(x) Zop y.

Wop/Zop are q3 Fourier multipliers. The varying coefficients multiply **after** inverse FFT; moving them inside the spectral multiplier discards heterogeneity coupling. Actual P is used directly, with no ideal circulant or commuting-phase assumption. For defect d after TSA4, set

    y0 = G^-1 Q*d,
    y1 = y0 - G^-1(C-G)y0,
    correction = U y1.

This frozen one-step map is complex linear and uses actual heterogeneous coefficients without a fine teacher. A sufficient convergence condition for repeated Neumann updates is `||G^-1(C-G)||<1`. A computable conservative upper estimate is

    ||G^-1|| [max|Delta t| max_k||W(k)|| + max|Delta s| max_k||Z(k)||].

An estimate >=1 leaves contraction unproved; it is not a failure certificate. Physical H coercivity does not transfer automatically to this oblique C. Its physical residual can be worse than raw least squares, although its different non-greedy outer directions could behave differently; only the unchanged full solver settles that tradeoff.

At N256, W and Z each add9MiB in complex128, on top of36MiB U. A joint forward FFT can feed the two q3 multipliers, followed by two q3 inverse transforms and local coefficient products; the final U lift still costs q3 forward/L12 inverse transforms. Charge all caches, construction, clones, helpers, physical checks and solver work. A reduced channel count alone is not evidence of cheaper wall time.

Exact rank3 Woodbury is **not** available for the full stored phase: higher angular phase harmonics are nonzero. Ideal direction-based tridiagonal/circulant reductions also change rounded stored data. Such approximations need original-physics residual refinement and separate gates; at L12 an exact rank12 Woodbury correction has no evident asymptotic setup advantage. [Fan–An–Ying](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.03061) already supplies angular reduction/homogeneous FFT/heterogeneous factorization precedents, so none of these mechanisms establishes priority.

## Full-angular control and the decision barrier

If evidence points to the response range itself rather than setup, a cheaper full-angular classical control is more informative than q5 or a new FNO. Reuse the existing mean inverse for one full-angle reference defect correction after a shallow TSA action, and compare it with the truncated response correction and a neighboring ordinary TSA depth at matched complete cost. This removes the q3 restriction without acquiring a full inverse teacher. Full-angle reference corrections already occur inside TSA; the tested distinction is the shallow mixed composition and its setup/action budget, not new transport physics.

Do not claim that an optimal source-specific least-squares correction supplies an optimal FGMRES preconditioner. Conversely, repeatedly trying oblique/learned coefficients after a failed cheap cached composition needs a concrete new cost or geometry hypothesis. Flat POD, radial/hierarchy compression and CT/NTO negatives already rule out recommending those names without a different representation and a feasible budget.

Keep Q1 primary. Q16 is only a conditional forecast until multiple distinct sources on the same material are actually measured. For a reusable candidate, account `T_offline + sum_m(S_candidate,m + Q_m T_candidate,m)` versus the strongest classical costs, crediting classical reuse as well. If measured online savings are nonpositive, training has no online amortization argument. [Peng ROMSAD](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.08735v3) and [Tang–Peng trajectory-aware ROM](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.05001v1) are genuine competitors for material/trajectory information; they do not remove acquisition/setup charges here.

A concrete scientific barrier is reached when the unchanged-physics classical Q1 controls are adequately covered, short-capture/reuse setup screens have no feasible budget, and the constrained proposed action/range has no charged cached headroom. That supports preserving a meaningful negative result for this specified L12/g.65/epsilon.1 workload. It does not justify broad claims about harder angular/absorption/boundary regimes or an endless campaign on the current fast cohort.
