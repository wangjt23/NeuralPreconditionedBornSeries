## Back to the actual research objective

The original target is a cheap material-conditioned nonlocal inverse action at real early states, not a more favorable second-injection schedule. Prior learned prefix4, global POD/KRR, first-coarse B8 composition and post-coarse short-stage variants are not reopened. The latest shell sequence separated three questions: exact support can remove work; its paid inverse is too costly; a fixed cheap substitute fails to replace heavy calls. That local-tuning line is closed.

The new object is A0(k)^{-1}, with all12 angular channels, ordered by physical normal position after fixing tangent Fourier mode. Its far-field blocks could in principle be represented independently of global source-response rank. Existing angular-band factors attack angular algebra but retain dense normal blocks. This would target an actual representation cost rather than add another outer direction.

Independent old-code audit found the previous hierarchy was a different object: a2D periodic quadtree on six even-parity angular channels of a general heterogeneous parity-Schur inverse. It was not this full-angle, fixed-mode,1D normal Green inverse. Existing HSS elsewhere means Hermitian/skew-Hermitian splitting, not the current hierarchical matrix. This distinction is scoped, not a claim that hierarchical RTE solvers are novel.

## Decision and exact limit of the exclusion

Stop this fixed rank12 normal-Green exact-replacement proposition. Do not enlarge rank, relax repair tolerance or launch all17 modes/full PDE/training to search for a favorable result. The test does not prove that all hierarchical matrices or all inexact preconditioners fail. An inexact proposal guarded by the original outer equation is a different contract and would need its own quality/cost argument, not a retrospective relabelling of this failure.

Before another numerical branch, a new representation must explain how it retains the original-operator-weighted response while avoiding paid dense inverse construction. A direct structured factorization or residual-weighted construction would be a new mathematical/algorithmic question, not permission to sweep ranks. There is currently no verified affordable witness to promote to GPU or learning. Keep existing CPU E and the separate captured compact4 GPU control; do not compare CPU seconds against GPU seconds as a speedup.

All owned workers exited; no GPU work, other-job interruption, paid-resource creation, production edits or Library upload occurred.
