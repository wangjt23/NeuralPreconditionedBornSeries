## What this establishes

The amplitude6 workload raises the total inner applications per four sources from19 in E's amplitude3 results to24 and raises the outer TSA64 calls from8 to10. E nevertheless retains a complete paid CPU advantage over the current compact4 control. Background identity helps attribute the extension to handling stronger local coupling rather than selecting a different background.

The scope remains the same fixed source family and the already declared inclusion-strength perturbation. It does not establish arbitrary material, angular-kernel, grid or source generalization. F is not promoted as a CPU timing winner, and no GPU conclusion is drawn from these CPU seconds. Earlier B, E, F and G evidence is preserved.

No further experiment follows automatically. A broader claim would require a new predeclared independent workload or a mathematical scope argument, rather than another amplitude sweep or a GPU retuning loop. The original stop conditions remain the appropriate boundaries for any future authorized gate.
