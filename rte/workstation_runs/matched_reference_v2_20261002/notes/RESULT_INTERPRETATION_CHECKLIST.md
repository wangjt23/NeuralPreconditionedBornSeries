# Raw-result interpretation checklist

Prepared before scientific v2 results, 2026-10-02. No new experiment, SSH, source export, training or Library mutation. The v1 capture failure is an engineering failure before science. Use the separately frozen v2 code/mask hashes and its successful parity evidence; never pool failed-v1 and successful-v2 rows as timing repeats.

1. **Establish what actually completed.** Verify settings/counts, unique context/method/repeat keys, all operator/ownership/cache-mask checks before full solves, finite original-H fields/images, declared process/memory limits and raw failures. The current frozen plan has252 checks, eight geometry targets, four truncated prefixes and160 full solves (120 measured/40 warmups); a v2 policy revision is the actual authority if its counts differ. Missing/early-converged geometry remains ineligible, not a bad range. Four-call prefix `failed` does not mean a complete-solver failure.

2. **Keep matched arithmetic evidence.** Full/q3 use one original FP32 cache, literal low defect/addition, identical physical H/start/tail budget, and only the input mask differs. Check each raw/segmented pair against its own literal eager recipe, with full field/status equality; full versus q3 may legitimately use different work. The cached mask repair must preserve exact0/1 values, dtype/device/layout guards and actual output/image parity. It does not improve physical precision or establish a q3-channel implementation.

3. **Interpret closure fields correctly.** `full_homogeneous_closure_relative`/q3 closure concern the promoted stored reference H0 and approximate cache/application. Physical-versus-reference differences belong to D=H-H0, combining heterogeneity and stored-operator differences. Record `low_vs_high_defect_relative`, additivity and real-AZ Gram/residual projection gaps before a causal interpretation. A small global field closure is not a max-frequency or global-operator certificate.

4. **Compute the declared signal from the actual metric.** For every grid and source/prefix context separately, reduce the two media's composite `best_real_line_remaining_ratio` to `mean(q3_ratio²-full_ratio²)`. The frozen positive full-information signal is>=.02 in each declared stratum, not a comparison of unsquared ratios or useful norms. Preserve individual signs, absolute norms, alpha, real cosine, unit ratios and increment/composite distinction. An opposite q3 advantage is descriptive evidence of filtering; do not invent a new post hoc gate. Geometry is not a speed claim.

5. **Keep the two-image fit narrow.** Report measured-image span rank/Gram spectrum/coefficients, attained ratio, and acquisition/fit cost. There is no independently H-verified fitted field combination and no full-space range optimum. A lower span ratio supports complementary measured images only. It cannot establish the q3 range floor, the full-input inverse ceiling, or a deployable nonlinear policy.

6. **Check actual economics and eligibility.** Use only accepted strong arms as comparators; freeze one correction backend/rule per grid by the declared two-media mean, and report same-backend full/q3 pairs too. Require the unchanged10% mean Q1 and10% mean warm-accepted target at both grids and the frozen worst-case limit. Charge only actually required setup, whole solve and independent acceptance; retain failed/slow/easy cases. First-use/common charges, constructor order and setup-plus-warm scope remain explicit. All successful classical controls strengthen the next denominator. Geometry cost is diagnostic unless an oracle is deployed; do not omit its acquisition then claim an oracle win.
