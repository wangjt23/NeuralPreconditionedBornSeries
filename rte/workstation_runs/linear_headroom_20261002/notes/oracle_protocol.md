No solve, training, GPU job, new fine teacher, held-out access, or baseline promotion was performed by this reviewer. Use the existing development parent only; seed 9626 remains reserved. ## Mechanism and why it is different

The preferred new hypothesis is **full-spatial, low-angular Galerkin correction of the actual heterogeneous operator**, with a cheaper full-angle classical complement. Begin with q=3 angular modes 0,+1,-1; q=5 is a predeclared diagnostic extension only if q=3 has inadequate range but measurable time headroom. Retain every original spatial Fourier mode and the stored Nyquist convention. The eventual learned target would approximate this material-dependent coarse inverse with an inexpensive material-frozen linear action; no new residual FNO is proposed.

Let U be an orthonormal L-by-q angular basis, acting independently at every spatial point, and let H denote the unchanged accepted FP64 physical A. Define

    Ac = U* H U
    z0 = Bcheap r
    d = r - H z0
    M r = z0 + U Ac^-1 U* d.

Bcheap is a frozen cheap full-angle control, initially TSA4; HSS4 is a secondary analytic control if needed, not a tuning campaign. This is a multiplicative correction, retaining the full complement. Ac uses the heterogeneous sigma_t and sigma_s fields, actual phase and all spatial frequencies. In a factored implementation, its local term is sigma_t(U*U)-sigma_s(U*P_actual U); its streaming term is the projection of the **actual H streaming action**. A q-channel FFT action may reduce angular work, but q² coupling, prolongation, fine defects, inner iterations and memory can erase that saving.

Direct `U* high.A_op(U c)` is the operator authority. Derive Mx/My only from the promoted stored direction/frequency factors used by that H, and verify the resulting action against the direct authority on fixed probes. Do not substitute a newly idealized phase, directions, frequencies, symmetrized operator, or the separately rounded low-precision streaming symbol from the previous precision ledger. Equal quadrature here permits the existing Euclidean adjoint convention; do not insert another 1/12 factor. Construct/orthonormalize U in complex128 and report its Gram defect.

With U*U=I and Re<x,Hx> >= m||x||², Ac inherits the same accretivity margin m. This establishes invertibility of the exact reduced operator. It does **not** establish contraction or cost advantage: the Galerkin residual projector I-HU Ac^-1 U* is oblique and can increase the full physical residual. Dropping higher angular modes also omits their Schur coupling, and angle-localized grazing errors may require many modes even though spatial frequencies are all retained. No automatic asymptotic or continuous-RTE guarantee follows.

An accurately converged inner FGMRES action is a classical/oracle control. Its RHS-dependent Krylov coefficients make it nonlinear, including when its iteration count is fixed. Outer real-stacked FGMRES can use it, but a residual-linear deployment later needs material-frozen factors, polynomial coefficients, or a fixed linear cycle; finite-precision linearity must also be audited. The notation Ac^-1 is the ideal target, not a description of a capped numerical solve.

This mechanism is distinct from those implementations, without a novelty claim. [Dölz–Palii–Schlottbom](https://arxiv.org/html/2102.09038v2) supplies a serious anisotropic angular-subspace classical precedent, under a different variational discretization. [Fan–An–Ying](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.03061) combines angular reduction with homogeneous FFT and heterogeneous skeletonization. [ROMSAD](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.08735v3) and [trajectory-aware ROM](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.05001v1) are correction-space/trajectory competitors, with different transport discretizations. [McMg](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.30495v2) demonstrates cached material-dependent residual-linear multilevel actions in Helmholtz; it does not establish that this q-channel RTE inverse is cheap or learnable. [Melchers et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.19867v3) motivates testing coarse coverage and compatible operator integration before fitting. Their results do not provide this RTE's convergence theorem.

## Bounded oracle: separate range, closure and cost

Use the **first two existing development media**, at N128/N256, both the original source and saved post-classical-prefix residual/frontier: at most eight contexts. Hash parent fields, sources, prefix state and ordered Krylov history. Reuse existing captured TSA prefixes; do not acquire a full fine solution. Retain all four material/grid pairs, including already-converged/easy prefixes. Nested grids are two independent media, not four. Repeated timings do not create new RHS or scientific samples. Use matrix-free coarse actions, never dense qN²-by-qN² assembly or inversion; q3 at N256 already has 196,608 complex unknowns.

1. **Projection parity before solves.** Check Ac against direct U*HU, adjoint consistency against direct H*, finite outputs and the inherited positive margin. The full H* must use the actual stored phase adjoint and sign-reversed actual streaming; do not assume rounded phase symmetry. Freeze dtype/operator/source hashes before measurements. An identity failure blocks interpretation and is repaired before further work.
2. **Accurate Galerkin control.** Solve Ac c=U*d with the same-q homogeneous reference inverse as the inner classical preconditioner. Require independently recomputed relative coarse residual <=1e-8 with a 100-direction cap. Record iterations and every coarse action. A capped/failed action is underresolved, not evidence that the angular range fails. Measure ||d-HU c|| and its useful image after projection against old AZ. No projection-only norm substitutes for the final physical residual.
3. **Image-range ceiling.** Separately minimize ||d-Jc||, where J=HU for a source context and J=(I-P_AZ)HU for a prefix context. P_AZ is the original **real** orthogonal image projector. Use real-stacked least squares and its actual adjoint, with a 100-iteration cap and predeclared 1e-8 relative normal-residual target; verify both objective and stationarity independently. This optimistic range oracle charges no learned prediction, but is explicitly not a deployable inverse. If the cap leaves it underresolved, report an attainable upper bound on minimum residual, not an “optimal” ceiling or a range impossibility. Even stationarity alone can be misleading without conditioning/gap evidence. For unprojected HU, inherited minimum singular value >=m gives the ordinary-arithmetic objective-gap bound ||J*(d-Jc)||²/m². The projected real operator need not retain that bound; do not reuse m after AZ removal.
4. **Continuation headroom.** Feed the frozen Galerkin or image-oracle proposal into the existing safeguarded joint image-space update and original ordered continuation. Time an offline-cached-proposal arm which includes proposal transfer/copy, every physical image, projection, acceptance and final physical check, but excludes acquisition. This isolates an optimistic inference budget. Include the same helper with a cheap homogeneous moment proposal, and the inference-free frozen portfolio. A range-quality improvement without saved full continuation time is a negative result. Source initialization is evaluated as an explicitly different start; its entire cost is charged.
5. **Actual cost only after a ceiling passes.** Evaluate at most one fixed q=3 actual Galerkin-corrected cheap-complement arm from the original source in the original solver. Keep the accurately solved classical version as a competitor; separately expose any fixed-cost approximation. At most one q=5 range extension is allowed under the range/headroom condition above. This protocol does not authorize learning or tuning a family.

The range oracle distinguishes inadequate angular coverage from a poor Galerkin closure. Accurate Galerkin success with slow inner solves instead identifies a possible amortized inverse target. Useful range but failed Galerkin closure suggests higher-moment Schur information is needed; increasing neural capacity on the same inverse target is not justified. None of these observations alone demonstrates economics.

## Cost budget and stopping decision

For each pair, let Tportfolio be contemporaneous original uninstrumented full-solve time and Tcached the complete cached-oracle solve time including common prefix/initialization and helper. Define the **optimistic chargeable budget**

    B = Tportfolio - Tcached.
    Required extra online cost + extra per-medium setup / Q < B.

If an oracle needs k correction calls, an average apply budget is at most B/k after other extras. This is the measured budget for that proposal and continuation path, not a theorem bounding all possible solver trajectories or mechanisms. Report per-case budgets and per-grid paired means, including easycases; positive eligible-only means cannot establish full-cohort savings. A near-perfect direction saving less than its cheapest plausible q-channel FFT, transfer and physical-defect cost closes this route without training. Do not estimate B by subtracting sums of synchronized profile categories: those profiles perturb the execution. Component timings explain cost; uninstrumented complete solves decide it.

Use the original solver without per-stage synchronization. After warmup, at most three balanced paired repeats on the four physical inputs are enough for this screening gate. Record raw order/times, setup, first use, peak memory, input/output casts, coarse calls and failures. No repeat-until-positive policy. Apply a soft 240-second process budget at safe boundaries, retaining incomplete receipts; unrelated job contention makes timing inconclusive and does not permit cancellation. This reviewer launches no jobs.

Continue this route only if an accurately verified cached range/Galerkin oracle has at least 10% paired mean warm headroom at **both** N128/N256, no physical failures, and no individual warm regression greater than 10%. This 10% is an exploratory margin to leave space for real inference/setup costs, not a measured benefit. The actual non-oracle candidate then needs at least 5% whole-solve saving at both grids, no case regression greater than 10%, no setup-plus-one-solve mean regression, and all accuracy gates. These two-media results authorize only further development, not a speedup/generalization claim. Underresolved numerical ceilings remain inconclusive; a complete nonpositive ceiling or inadequate actual economics is retained as a negative result.

For Q distinct RHS per medium, compare totals

    Classical: sum_m [Sc,m + sum_j tc,mj]
    Learned: Toffline + Tload + sum_m [Sl,m + sum_j tl,mj].

Toffline includes actual oracle/data acquisition, failures, training and selection; Sl includes encoding/factors, verification and cache refresh. Credit reusable setup on both sides. With positive average per-RHS saving Delta t, break-even requires Q Delta t > (Sl-Sc) + allocated offline/loading cost. If Delta t<=0 and setup is no cheaper, increasing Q cannot help. Warm repetitions of one source do not justify this amortization. Until distinct-source reuse and practical coarse action are demonstrated, one-source setup-plus-solve economics are the decision criterion.

**Recommendation:** perform the bounded q=3 actual-heterogeneous angular range/closure/cost oracle, alongside the parent's unchanged-classical launch headroom check. It is a concrete different mechanism with a small number of interpretable outcomes. Retain all old negatives; start no FNO retraining or coarse-learning campaign before the available budget is positive and large enough to pay for a specified action.
