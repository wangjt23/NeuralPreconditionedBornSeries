## Mathematical and admissibility requirements

For fixed exact complex-linear H and right action M, unrestarted real-coefficient Krylov corrections lie in the real span of the same powers of HM; the containing complex span cannot have a larger optimal residual at equal degree. This says nothing about measured time, changed restarted trajectories, flexible rounded actions or the N128 residual-conditioned injection. Complex coefficients do not repair a demonstrated phase defect: the original real-stacked method is already globally phase-equivariant when the complete action/state/injection is covariant.

Actual flexible-GMRES validity needs H to map a complex combination of retained solution directions to the corresponding combination of their physical images. M need not be exactly complex-linear for every flexible-GMRES use. Nevertheless, the proposed fixed-action mechanism requires a nominal complex-linear M and a prespecified floating-point admissibility check. Test actual H and deployed M on frozen representative source, grazing/mixed and deterministic random-complex probe pairs; record finite output, general complex addition/scaling, multiplication by i, nontrivial phase and real scaling, in both field and original-H image, using denominators protected against zero. Keep H-only versus M versus H∘M diagnostics distinct. Root/math approved engineering caps are high H/native128 ≤1e−12, low H64 ≤2e−6, G/R0_64 ≤5e−6, and actual backcast64 strong eager/direct-segmented ≤1e−4; the newly proposed **H∘deployed composite cap is 1e−3**, separately declared before GPU and required in the math note/policy. It permits FP32 field discrepancy amplified by physical H and does not relax H-only's 1e−12 cap. Exact zero and independent own-literal parity remain separate checks. Exact metric normalizers must be frozen in policy. These caps are not uniform error bounds. Native128 helper checks must be distinguished from the actual solver's FP32-backcast action. A finite probe pass supports those evaluations, not global exact linearity. Any failure blocks this prespecified screen; do not change precision, Nyquist convention, operator, threshold or factor to rescue it. Rounding alone need not invalidate every complex flexible solver.

The complex augmented implementation requires conjugate MGS coefficients, conjugate-transpose QR projections, complex tiny least squares, and rank handling over C. Real-independent columns can be complex-dependent (w and iw). Preserve a teacher image in span(V) but outside span(AZ): this can be a useful happy-breakdown direction, not a reject-by-frontier test. Reject nonfinite/zero/dependent or physically worsening injected proposals while keeping the original ordered Arnoldi frontier. Store actual solution directions Z with stable owned storage; a replay's static output may not alias older Z. Final residuals must use original H and rhs, not only Hessenberg estimates. Match the real baseline's one-MGS convention and **absolute next-frontier cutoff 1e−14**. The restored absolute cutoff was independently verified in the current helper. It uses SVD for each complex tiny LS and stops on complex-rank loss after physically checking the attained candidate, rather than carrying an invalid AZ-QR frontier. These safeguards differ from original real CUDA lstsq, are scientifically defensible explicit complex-rank safety, and must be disclosed/charged rather than described as identical tiny-LS arithmetic or a pure coefficient-only implementation comparison.

Any optional frozen-span diagnostic uses unprojected W=HZ and actual reconstructed fields. Complex LS on a pre-existing real-AZ projection is not an equivalent test: its projector generally does not commute with i. No coercivity lower bound transfers through that real projection. Such diagnostics must be separately timed and cannot create an unbilled teacher or justify broader training.
