## Primary one-RHS and conditional Q16 cost gates

For material i and declared RHS count Q, define complete classical costs

    Ceager_i(Q) = Scommon_i + Seager_i + sum_j teager_ij
    Creplay_i(Q)= Scommon_i + Seager_i + Scapture_i + sum_j treplay_ij
    Cstar_i(Q)  = min(Ceager_i(Q), Creplay_i(Q)).

This lower envelope is a conservative benchmark target; it does not claim that an unmeasured adaptive backend selector is free. Charge only the setup required by each recipe, credit valid shared caches once, and disclose any backend-selection overhead in a deployable method. Scommon includes the same physical/source construction and other shared work on both sides. Include startup/loading and first-use work if claiming process-cold cost; if only recorded setup plus warm latency is available, label the result an estimate rather than measured complete cold latency.

**Q=1 is primary.** For a cached candidate continuation of backend b, let Ccached_i(1) include all its actual selected classical setup plus clone/helper/full-solve cost, but exclude the separately disclosed oracle acquisition. Its optimistic available budget is

    Bi(1) = Cstar_i(1) - Ccached_i(1).

Any actual candidate's additional material setup, acquisition/prediction/loading and online work must fit inside Bi. For a 5% complete-cost opportunity the stricter budget is `.95*Cstar_i(1)-Ccached_i(1)`. A graph-only candidate may have zero one-RHS budget even with a perfect warm direction because capture alone is expensive; retain an eager continuation arm if the mechanism is intended for one source. Do not conclude algorithmic failure from a mandatory replay backend that was already unsuitable for this workload.

For Q=16, report only the **conditional** estimate with each method's reusable setup paid once. Substitute 16 times the measured saved-source warm latency only as an explicit equal-latency forecast. Source-dependent iteration counts, rank/shape and cache reuse can change. Global-phase aliases or repeated timings of one source are not sixteen distinct-RHS workload evidence, and sixteen different materials do not amortize one medium's setup. Genuine distinct-source evaluation would be a separate authorized development study.

For learning, add all actual teacher/data/failed-acquisition/training/tuning cost and model loading. If Delta t is a positive average per-distinct-RHS saving, break-even requires `Q Delta t > extra_material_setup + allocated_offline_and_loading_cost`. When online cost is slower and setup no cheaper, larger Q cannot repair economics. Do not hide the source-specific CGLS solves as free offline work for a new medium.

Recommend a provisional **10% cached complete-cost headroom screen** at both N128/N256, zero physical failures and no individual complete-cost regression above 10%, leaving room for actual correction costs. Preserve positive but smaller findings as exploratory evidence without opening training. A practical actual-action candidate must then achieve at least 5% complete one-RHS saving at both grids with all accuracy gates. Existing 10% warm gates remain separate measurements; passing them cannot override the primary complete-cost failure. These two-media thresholds are screening rules, not significance or generalization claims.

## Outcome interpretation and bounded next scope

* A positive finite attained proposal with accurate full continuation is a feasible information/budget witness, even if CGLS remains capped. It is not an optimal inverse or deployable learned solver.
* A loose raw bracket or capped projected solve leaves optimum range quality unresolved. Negative timings close the tested attained source/tail recipe, not every coefficient field in range(U).
* A tight raw bracket plus no meaningful cached benefit constrains this raw range on these cases. It does not rule out an earlier/multiplicative full-complement trajectory.
* Positive cached budget with acquisition/setup larger than that budget identifies an expensive target; it does not establish that a network can approximate it cheaply.
* Any physical-accuracy failure blocks cost promotion. No tolerance, field gate, failure accounting or reserved-test policy changes after results.

A from-start TSA4-plus-response composition is mathematically distinct and can be considered next only under a newly frozen bounded protocol: `M=M4+U(HU)†(I-HM4)`, retaining the full complement and charging every defect/coefficient solve/action. Exact LS gives residual nonincrease relative to M4; finite CGLS and cost do not inherit a speed guarantee. Do not silently perform that broader repeated-action study or FNO training after the current one-shot gate fails. The mechanism note ranks a genuinely cheaper full-angular cached replacement ahead of another late TSA16-alignment campaign, conditional on a representation-specific cost oracle.
